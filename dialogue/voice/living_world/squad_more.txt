# Squad mates: our own lines and scenes, on top of the pack's (squad.txt). Read by build_dialogue.py, used by squad_pack.lua.
#
#   +id|tags|who|text          who: empty = any mate, or key=a/b;key=c  with keys
#                                role   tank (PLD NIN)  healer (WHM)  hybrid (RDM)  support (BRD COR)  damage (the rest)
#                                voice  bold calm cheerful gruff (the mate's voice from squad_dialogue.lua)
#                                fam    new (below 20)  steady (20-49)  trusted (50+): how far the mate has levelled
#                                race   Hume Elvaan Tarutaru Mithra Galka      gender  male female
#   +scene:id|tag|cooldown     then one  role|text  per line (roles tank healer damage support; support = any non-healer)
#
# When each use plays (squad_pack.lua): s.battle at the pull, s.lowhp from the mate under 30% HP, s.lowmp from a healer
# under 20% MP, s.after when a fight ends and s.reply from a second mate, s.idle_town / s.idle_field / s.banter in quiet
# moments (fight and quiet lines only while the squad chat AI is quiet). On call: s.recruit s.wipe s.jobchange s.reunion
# s.bond s.race. s.down when the mate is knocked out, s.ack_hold / s.ack_follow on !squad camp, s.bench on dismiss.
# Keep lines true whatever happened: no counts of monsters, no who-pulled-what, no weather, no named city.
# Plain ASCII, no contractions (the squad's voice), at most about 110 characters.
