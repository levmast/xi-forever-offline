Sprites for the launcher's 8-Bit theme
======================================

These are sprites from "Records of Vana'diel", a pack of FINAL FANTASY XI characters drawn as 8 / 16-bit console
sprites. Its maker sent it to Hurami for this theme (see CREDITS.md for the credit).

Only the pictures the theme can use are here, renamed from the pack's own folders:
    battle\P001 ... P006    the battle sprites, 32 x 32 (Default, Ready, RightAttack, SkillReady, Win, Damage, Dying, Down ...)
    map\P001 ... P006       the map sprites, 16 x 16 (Default, WalkL, WalkB, RightHandUp, LookDown, Down)
P004 is not here: it is left out until it is settled which character she is, because the project's art stays within the
classic era, up to Treasures of Aht Urhgan. The pack's game files (the bundles and the UnityPlayer.dll it came with)
are not here either and are no part of this project.

art-src\scripts\bit_buttons.py draws the theme's buttons (assets\press\8bit-ui) with these sprites:
    knight = P001, thief = P002, monk = P003, white = P005, black = P006

FINAL FANTASY XI and its characters are the property of Square Enix Co., Ltd.
