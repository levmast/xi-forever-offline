# XI: Forever Offline patch notes

Newest first. The launcher shows this file on SYSTEM > Patch notes; the same notes go to #patch-notes on Discord.

## v0.3.12 · 26 November 2026

### New
• A new launcher theme: Windurst. The launcher as a storybook of the Federation of the Stars: rounded frames of Star Tree wood with Windurst's green, gold stars, and Windurst's own people on every button (Shantotto on PLAY, Tarutaru and Mithra, the mages, Cardians, Mandragora and a Yagudo on STOP). Seven banners of its own: Windurst, the heroes of the Federation, the Star Onion Brigade, Tarutaru and Mithra, the ministries of magic, the Starlight Celebration and Sarutabaruta. The PLAY cards get Windurst borders too. It's the launcher's standard look now, with its own song by JØ, our Head of Music; Chronicle, Aero and the others are still on OPTIONS > Theme (and if you picked one there, you keep it).
• VanaSync, battle music by JØ, our Head of Music (VanaSync by JØ × NOX). Off by default: switch on "vanasync" on OPTIONS > Addons. While it's on and your world runs, a small VanaSync player window opens, and fights get JØ's battle themes: "Against the Odds" when you're alone, "Beyond the Veil" in a party and "The March of Many" in an alliance. The game's own music fades out only while a VanaSync song really plays and comes back 5 seconds after the fight; sound effects and voices stay as they are. Close the window or type /vanasync restore and the game's music is back. More of JØ's music (zones, his album Resonance of Vana'diel) is coming in later packs. Windows only for now.
• XIFO app 1.4.0: Tetra Master in the app (CARDS on the taskbar), with your character's own cards from the game. It opens once Luto Mewrilah in Upper Jeuno has given that character the starter deck. Play the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard with the game's own rules: a win pays the game's gil (a perfect game double, times your world's Tetra Master gil rate) and a card that goes up a class keeps it; both reach your character at your next PLAY, like phone fishing. Or press Find an opponent and duel whoever else is looking: the hub runs the match (45 seconds a turn), checks you play cards you really own, and keeps a win / loss record. As in the game, no cards change hands. Nalfey's board, cards and sounds.
• XIFO app 1.4.0: a new Dark Fantasy look, now the default: black and dusk-violet stone, old-gold edges and Cinzel lettering under a pale moon. The other looks are still in Start > Look & About.
• XIFO app 1.4.1: sharper Ventures squad mates. Kaza's heroes are drawn at their full size now (they were shrunk before, which blurred the outlines, eyes and trim), the hair and skin colours fit them pixel for pixel, and they move more: a breathing idle, a full walk, attacks that fit the job (sword swings, thrusts for Monk, Dragoon, Puppetmaster and Beastmaster, shots for Ranger and Corsair, a raised hand and a cast for mages), dodging and taking hits up front, and a victory pose.
• XIFO app 1.3.0: portable fishing fishes by itself now. Pick a spot, your rod and bait, press Start fishing, and your character casts, waits for a bite, fights and lands its catch at about the game's pace (a cast every half minute or so), a little less often than a careful hand would in game. It keeps going while the app is closed too: when you open it again, the time is fished out (up to 4 hours, while the bait lasts, and never past the game's 200 catches a day) and you get a summary. Your rod and bait come from your character's own gear, shown like the Profile's equipment (what's in your Range and Ammo slots is picked first). The arrows minigame is gone.
• XIFO app 1.3.0: fishing stays first person (just your rod over the water), and 60 fishing spots have the painted landscapes from Kaza's art above the water. Ventures got hit sparks and damage numbers that float, casters glowing as they cast, the monster fading out with the exp it gave, dust on the road, a campfire when the party rests, sparkles at the homecoming, and the light of the Vana'diel hour outdoors.
• XIFO app 1.2.2: sound for portable fishing and Ventures. Chiptune effects (the cast, a bite, every tap of the fight, a catch, a lost fish, skill-ups, sending a venture off and the homecoming) and a music loop for each, all made for the app. Sounds and Music switches on both screens.
• Tetra Master: /tm volume 0-100 (default 40) sets how loud the card sounds and the music are. Thanks Jaymz.
• The Hub: you keep your own rates there. Your experience, drops, gil, skill-ups, crafting, fishing, digging and Tetra Master rates come with you from your own world, so friends on 1x and 10x can party together and each gets their own. A monster's drops and gil use the rate of whoever claimed it. Fights stay the same for everyone (monster HP, damage, your HP and MP). Steel and Silver characters keep their own rates as always. Other open worlds still play visitors at the host's rates.
• Iron and Randomized characters play at the rates of the world they're in: their own profile at home, the host's rates when visiting. Steel and Silver keep their own rates in every world.
• One PLAY screen: PLAY shows a card for each of your characters, with their portrait, job, level and where they are, and a border for what it is: regular, Iron, Steel, Silver, Randomized, or a second-window (dualbox) character, with the Hardcore shields, in every launcher look. Pick one and you go straight into the game as that character, with no character list; a Hardcore or Randomized card plays with its own mode's rules, like PLAY HARDCORE did. Pick a SECOND WINDOW card first and it opens beside the main character you pick (or straight away while you're playing). CREATE A CHARACTER asks, on cards: Normal, Hardcore or Randomized; for Hardcore, Iron, Steel or Silver; for Randomized, permadeath or not, and whether your squad is randomized too; then main window or second window, and the name. With it, the HARDCORE and DUALBOX buttons are gone (the Hardcore list is the "Hardcore and Randomized characters" link under PLAY). Untick "Show this at PLAY" to get the old PLAY, HARDCORE and DUALBOX back. Needs the new server (the login server now always lists your characters in the same order).
• Randomized with permadeath: a Randomized character made with permadeath falls like Silver. Knocked out once, it's gone, rests on the memorial and is deleted after 7 days. Its card says PERMA RANDOMIZED, and a fallen one shows FALLEN.
• !skip, for GMs: skip story missions you've done before, on any character. Log in on your GM account, have the character you're skipping for logged in too (a second window), and name it: !skip nation Hurami (its nation's missions and rank 10), !skip rank, !skip fame (the highest fame in San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst and Norg), !skip zilart, !skip promy (Promathia up to and including the Promyvions), !skip cop, !skip toau, !skip wotg, or !skip all (nation, rank, fame, Zilart, Promathia and Aht Urhgan). With no name it's the GM character itself. It shows what it would do first; add yes to do it (!skip promy Hurami yes). Skipped missions are marked done and the character gets their key items; the final rewards (rings, gear) aren't given. Hardcore and Randomized characters can't be skipped. It's off in open worlds like The Hub.
• Squad mates wait outside towns: walk into a town and the mates who were out stay outside, then rejoin you by themselves in the next area that isn't a town, with no call timer (like your Mog House). You can still call them in town by hand. !squad town off brings them into towns with you again. Thanks Neet.
• Squad teleports by party chat: say "d2" (or "warp ii") and your Black Mage casts Warp II on you, next to the six Teleports ("tele holla", "dem", "mea"...) and Escape. Thanks JØ.
• Squad Summoners that play like a player, by wildcabbagepatch (experimental): summons cost MP, avatars drain it the way yours do, Blood Pacts follow your rules and costs (the recast only starts when a pact goes off) and wait for the avatar to be ready and in reach, party pacts can be aimed at you, and each mate has its own settings and pact order (Gambits: a Summoner section and a Basic Summoner starter). Also a weapon skill gear set per mate: armor and accessories it wears from the start of a weapon skill until the damage lands.
• A second gift: the Forever Patch, an eyepatch in the Forever Beta Shirt's colours, with a big crystal and FOREVER BETA on the patch. Fishing skill +1 while you wear it, and use it for the shirt's experience bonus (+75%, up to 4000 exp, 12 hours; 5 charges, 15 hours apart); using it doesn't take away the fishing bonus. It's in your delivery box at your next login, once per character; new characters start with it.
• A gift for everyone: a Chocobo Egg in your delivery box at your next login, once per character (Hardcore and Randomized characters too). Keep it warm.
• Timed NMs keep their timers between play sessions. RULES > Comforts, "Timed NMs between sessions": 1 = paused while your servers are off (the default: a timer goes on where it stopped), 2 = real time (timers keep running while you're away), 0 = fresh timers at every start, as before. Argus and Leech King keep whose turn it is. Lottery NMs follow the lottery settings, the land kings their own switch. Thanks Trinity, Akiraheaven and VRTRA.
• Start the world over: SYSTEM > Settings, "Start the world over..." (type ERASE). Every character, squad mate, bot player, auction listing, linkshell and record goes; your game logins, the launcher's settings and add-ons stay, and a full copy of the old world is saved first (backups\world). Thanks Rennie, K-Andy and datadecay.
• Journal 0.4.6 by Kxteel (quests and missions, the pinned tracker, Vana'dex): the launcher now downloads and installs it itself from Kxteel's GitHub, still off until you install it (OPTIONS > Addons). The era edits (only what this world has, up to Aht Urhgan) go on by themselves.
• HD packs and music: Jasmint's HD Textures (players in HD) and MoonIcon-Tactics by Gdragoon91 (Final Fantasy Tactics style status and menu icons, lettering, cursor and menu colours) are in the list. Download them from Nexus Mods with your own account; the launcher installs them. The files that hold the Hardcore and Randomized marks are left out of any pack, so those marks always show. The Ultra HD ReShade look is listed with what it needs (installed by hand).

### Fixes
• Ventures: the exp, gil and items your mates bring home now reach the game. The game's part of Ventures was missing from the v0.3.11 server, so nothing arrived. Nothing is lost: finished ventures wait on xiforever.com until your game takes them in. Thanks Hasuko, Lunali and Kluze.
• Ventures: a second venture sent before you play again starts from the level the first one reached, not the old one. The app shows those levels too.
• Ventures: a Randomized character's random squad shows up in the app with the looks Fate rolled.
• Living world: the map server could crash when a bot player logged out right as it was answering someone in town (mostly seen on Linux, after 5 to 30 minutes in a busy town). Thanks Grim, Red, Zuken and mark devlin.
• Squad mates' bolts, arrows and bullets now really give their additional effects, such as Acid Bolt's Defense Down. v0.3.11 handed the real ammo over, but a second check still let only players' ammo effects through. Thanks Syfin and Watts.
• Portable fishing: the rods that can also land legendary fish (Lu Shang's, Ebisu, Judges) are in the app now, so a character holding one sees all its rods. Thanks Hasuko.
• !squad give <spell> finds a spell scroll by the spell's name ("!squad give sneak"), not only as "scroll of sneak". Thanks jainfarstrider.
• Treasure Hunter is era again: your own Thief's hits no longer raise it during a fight. It stays at what your trait and gear give (TH1 at 15, TH2 at 45, plus gear), the same as a squad Thief. Two Thieves still don't stack; the monster keeps the highest.
• Hardcore: your squad mates earn exp at your Hardcore mode's rate (3.5x on Silver, for example), not the world's normal rate. Thanks Teutonic84.
• Squad mates take Yagudo Drinks like juices (Refresh for 3 minutes). Thanks Hasuko.
• XIUIplus installs again: its team moved the downloads to a new page (github.com/Team-XIUI/XIUIplus), and the launcher was still looking in the old place. Thanks Hexblood, Dermit and gisela.
• Second window (dualbox): the add-ons you tick for it now load when it opens. Its script never loaded Ashita's own add-on loader, so only plugins came up; pressing DUALBOX puts it in. Thanks Ricc.
• Online features on some Windows PCs (Hardcore news to the Hub, character pages, chat, downloads): an old, expired Let's Encrypt certificate left in Windows' intermediate store made the launcher refuse xiforever.com with "certificate has expired", while browsers worked. The launcher now checks against Windows' trusted roots and Mozilla's list only.
• Squad Rangers (and any mate with a bow, gun or throwing weapon) keep shooting at mid and far range. They shot once and then stood there, because a squad mate's shots only reached 14 yalms and those spots are 15 and 20 yalms out; they now reach 25, like yours. Thanks Frost and Norrec.
• Full merits (RULES > Comforts, off by default) now really gives you everything: every merit starts at its maximum, with nothing to spend. Before, the server allowed it but the game's own merit menu still greyed merits out once their group looked full (Max HP 8/8 left Max MP grey). Switch it off again and you're back to the merits you bought. Thanks krazy.
• The Hub no longer opens your bags: a visitor keeps the bags they brought from home. Before, a Hardcore or classic-bags character came home from the Hub with every bag and wardrobe open. Thanks delukard.
• Launcher music: the volume slider works on PCs where the song plays through Windows Media Foundation (the ones that showed "MCI error 277" before), not only when the song starts. Thanks Hexblood.

## v0.3.11 · 25 November 2026

### New
• Ventures: send squad mates off to level up while you're away, from the XIFO app (phone, PC app or xiforever.com/app). Pick one mate or several (together they hunt as one party, with bot players filling the gaps), a real era camp that fits their level, and 1, 2, 4, 8 or 12 hours. The timer runs even with your PC off. Every mate earns 3,000 exp an hour (up to level 75, the same in every mode), plus gil and a modest share of the camp's drops and crystals. When it's over, the app shows the trip as postcards and a short story, and the next time you play the exp and gil reach your mates and the items arrive in your delivery box (from "Venture"). A mate who is away can't be called until it's back (or you call it home early in the app). Ventures from inside the game are coming too. Needs the app paired with your launcher (Characters page). The friends in the parties are Kaza's "Records of Vana'diel" sprites; thanks Kaza!
• XIFO app 1.2: the Ventures tab, and a new standard look, "Online 2003" (a dark early-2000s chat-room style); the earlier looks stay under Start > Look & About.
• Squads menu 1.32 and Gambits 0.3.7, by wildcabbagepatch: one shared look for both (Classic, Squad and Crimson themes, plus size and opacity), one card per mate with a Rules line that shows the rules each mate really runs, a Profile window with Stats and Gear views, and Save & Apply for a single mate in the Gambits editor (the others stay out). The launcher button has a Squads half and a Gambits half.
• Squad Summoners, the full job: pick a mate's avatar (!squad avatar [mate] [avatar|auto], or say "Ifrit", "all Shiva", "Ayame Garuda", "release" in party chat). On Auto it picks the avatar the target is weakest to. Summoners now use ward pacts (healing, buffs, Slowga and Lunar Cry), use Astral Flow on a notorious monster (or when you say "2hr"), and no longer pay twice for a Blood Pact.
• Random squad: on the first roll every mate gets a different race.

### Fixes
• Squad mates' bolts, arrows and bullets now really give their additional effects (Acid Bolt's Defense Down and the like). The v0.3.7 fix looked at the wrong copy of the ammo. Thanks Syfin and Watts.
• Graphics page: the draw distances are kept when you close the launcher, not only when you press PLAY. Thanks Dugahst.

## v0.3.10 · 24 November 2026

### New
• Randomized mode, round 4:
  - Fated monsters: when a Randomized character takes on a normal monster, 1 time in 50 Fate marks it. It becomes "Fated <name>", grows to full size, has half again its HP and hits 15% harder. Beat it for a guaranteed jackpot (a rare item of the area near its level, or an NM's treasure of about its level), announced to the area, the hub and Stinkshell.
  - Random squad (off by default): !squad random on, or the button on the character's card in the HARDCORE menu. Each squad mate rolls its own looks and its own random job, and gets new random jobs at 18, 33 and 65, like you. Off brings your mates back as they were; on again brings the same rolls back.
  - Milestones: every 10 levels on a job, a Randomized character gets 3 random cosmetics in the delivery box, from Fate.
  - Fairer random drops: a random drop is as rare as what it replaces and fits the monster's level (10 below to 5 above). NMs give what NMs of about their level give, so a low NM no longer drops level 70 gear.
  - Rangers start with a Shortbow and 99 Wooden Arrows; Corsairs with 99 Bronze Bullets and a Firefly (worn from level 5).
• Squad backup and restore: on the Squads page, "Back up squad" saves a character's whole squad to one file (every mate's look, jobs, levels, gear, unlocked items, which Rule Set each job uses, personality and greetings). "Restore..." puts it back, on the same character or another one; the squad it replaces is saved first. Thanks Rennie.
• Updates show their download size ("Download: about 410 MB") before you start. Thanks Jaymz.
• GM command !skipmissions <log> <mission> (for example !skipmissions cop THE_ROAD_FORKS): marks every earlier mission in that log done, makes the one you name your current mission, and gives the key items those missions hand out. Nation rank and final reward gear stay as they are (use !setrank and !additem). Needs a GM character. Thanks Blitzace, Quaiton and Mikoto.
• New Discord forum: #squad-personalities, for sharing your squad mates' personalities and backstories. Thanks AerynGunn.
• Character transfer now brings your quests and missions (every log, current and finished, CoP, ToAU, Assault and Campaign included), your currencies (conquest points, seals, tabs, assault points...) and your merits and merit points. Linkshells and pearls stay behind: they belong to the other game's linkshells. Thanks Hasuko.

### Fixes
• Bot players: a healer synced down by level sync kept trying spells above its synced level and never healed. Bots now only pick spells their job can cast at their current level. Thanks Mikoto.
• Throwing away a linkshell brought over by a character transfer crashed the server. Thanks Hasuko.
• XIUI: loot pop-ups no longer show twice or three times when a squad mate is called or zones. Thanks Hasuko.
• Each server update saves a copy of your database first. Only the newest 3 copies are kept now; the older ones are removed (they only piled up).
• Hardcore icons: the "!hcicons" line no longer shows in your chat when you zone. The server always answered it; the line was only the game's own copy of what the add-on asks (hcicons 1.6). Thanks Rysan, notsensible and everyone who reported it.
• GM commands on a logged-out character: giving an item (!giveitem) is now handed over at that character's next log in. It said "could not be" before. Thanks Ricc.
• HARDCORE menu: a Randomized character can now take the name of a character you deleted, as the game's own character creation already allows. Thanks datadecay.
• DUALBOX works right after setup_dualbox.py, without restarting the launcher first. Thanks Miniberg.
• setup_gm.py promote: a character already at that GM level is no longer reported as "not found". Thanks Keithvalentine.
• Character transfer: "is logged in" after a crash or a hard server stop. The import now clears the leftover login itself when the server is not running; no Repair needed. Thanks Hasuko.
• Launcher music: on PCs where Windows' old player fails ("MCI error 277"), the theme now plays through Windows Media Foundation instead. Thanks lilibun, Hexblood and Sterling.
• Your own .gguf AI model: the import now starts Ollama's service itself when it is not running, and shows a readable error if something else goes wrong. Thanks AerynGunn.
• PLAY: no more "FINAL_FANTASY_XI.msi" / "network resource that is unavailable" box when the game you copied from was deleted or moved. The launcher puts the copy's FFXi.dll back where Windows expects it. Don't uninstall FINAL FANTASY XI from Windows' Apps list: the copy needs those entries.
• Server: a character that had already left a zone could still be counted as leaving it on the zone's next tick. Thanks wildcabbagepatch.
• Co-op page: no more "'BooleanVar' object is not callable" box every few seconds; if it happens it is only noted in logs\coop-error.log. Thanks Jker_s81.
• Character transfer: a capture with an unusual character size (from some other servers) no longer stops the import ("Out of range value for column 'size'"). Thanks Jker_s81.
• San d'Oria mission 6-1: after the cutscene, a chat line now says which key item King Destin wants (a Dreamrose) and where to find it (Western Altepa Desert). Thanks lilibun.

## v0.3.5 · 23 November 2026

### New
• New installs start on the "era default" set: Fields/Grounds pages experience 0.128, treasure caskets 15%, era fishing (no minigame), guild shops that never run out, auction house from anywhere, Ancestry Moogles, 3 minutes between seals, the auction house simulator on, no training dummies, and 300 bot players with up to 180 online. An install you already have keeps every setting it has.
• HARDCORE, beside DUALBOX: Iron, Steel and Silver characters. Pick a mode on the new menu before your character's first level; its rates are locked to that mode's profile wherever it goes (co-op worlds and The Hub too). Every mode: no auction house, no bazaars, no trades or deliveries with other characters, squad gear only by trading it to your mates. Iron (1x experience) parties with anyone and can step down to a regular character. Steel (2x) and Silver (3.5x): no Home Point or Survival Guide teleports, parties only with hardcore characters (your squad, no bot players), and they can step down to Iron. A Steel character that is knocked out sends that job back to level 1 (everything you completed stays, squad mates keep their levels). A Silver character that is knocked out is gone: it rests on the memorial and is deleted 7 days later. Every 10 levels on a job: random cosmetics in your delivery box, 1 for Iron, 2 for Steel, 3 for Silver. Milestones, Steel falls and Silver deaths are announced in your world, on The Hub and in Stinkshell (switch it off in the Hardcore menu). Each mode's shield shows beside the character's name, drawn by the game itself like the bazaar mark, so everyone sees it (also on The Hub), and hosts can take hardcore visitors only. !hardcore shows your run.
• RANDOMIZED, the fourth card on the HARDCORE menu: you pick only the name, and there is no character creation screen. The launcher makes the character: Fate rolls your race, face, size and nation (and which of its three cities you start in), then one of the 18 jobs at your first login, and you arrive dressed in a random set of level 1 gear for that job with 10,000 gil in your pocket; another random job comes at 18, 33 and 65, and those are the only jobs that character can play (job quests still unlock jobs for your squad mates). Every drop from your claim is a random item that fits the area, and NMs drop from one shared NM pool; quest items, crystals, seals and Rare/Ex items stay as they are. Its own rates (2.5x experience, no experience lost on death), the auction house and trading allowed, normal deaths, and it can join any party, Steel and Silver included. Randomized characters may visit hardcore-only worlds. Treasure chests, coffers and caskets are random too (the area's items), and battlefield crates give what fights of the same level cap give. Its own auction house: the stock changes every 6 hours (crystals are always there) and each item's price swings up to 20% more than usual.
• Mode titles, always worn while you play the mode: Iron "Forged in Solitude", Steel "Tempered by Ruin", Silver "Bearer of a Single Flame", Randomized "Altana's Wild Card". Titles you earn still go on your list; they just stay off until you step down. The launcher adds the four names to the game's title list at PLAY.
• A new XI: Forever new-player mark: the red ? beside new characters' names is now a crystal one (it comes and goes as before). Iron, Steel, Silver and Randomized characters show their shield instead. The launcher puts the four shields and the new mark into the game's icon sheet at PLAY.
• Hardcore icons in the party list: a small column beside your party list shows each member's mode. Place it once with /hcicons party move (it fits the game's party list or XIUI's); /hcicons party row N lines it up.
• Squads menu: the Job and Sub lists show each job's level, like WHM (34). (Dugahst)
• Run speed: the running animation now speeds up with it, the way Flee does, instead of sliding. (Ricc)
• RULES > Skills and crafting: Chocobo digging skill (1x = the era). How much each find counts toward your digging skill; there is still no daily digging limit on your own world. (delukard)
• The Vaporwave look stays: after its limited time ends on December 2 it is a theme you can pick on the Theme page for good, with its song and banners. (Ranyxia)
